Jeffrey Epstein (January 20 1953 – August 10 2019) was an American financier who became widely known for his wealth, social connections, and criminal activities. After a career in finance and investment, he founded the financial services firm J. Epstein & Co. in 1982, managing assets for a small number of high‑net‑worth clients.
